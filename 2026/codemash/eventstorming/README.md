## Additional Resources

- [Introducing EventStorming](https://www.eventstorming.com/book/) - the EventStorming book by Alberto Brandolini - though unfinished, there are plenty of gems of information covered here
- [The EventStorming Handbook](https://leanpub.com/eventstorming_handbook) - by Paul Rayner, the lead organizer of Explore DDD - also unfinished and also full of nuggets of guidance
- [EventStorming.com](https://www.eventstorming.com/)
- [Alberto Brandolini - 50,000 Orange Stickies Later - Explore DDD 2017](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1i6QYvYhlYQ)
- [Alberto Brandolini - 100,000 Orange Stickies  Later - Øredev 2019](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fGm62ra_mQ8)

## Sadukie's EventStorming Webinars

- [EventStorming for the Win!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=B51yRroJkwE) - This one shows some of the tips & tricks I use when I am facilitating an EventStorming session online using Miro.
- [How EventStorming Streamlines Complex Domains](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iF_znCiF7KM) - In this webinar, I walk through an EventStorming session using an eCommerce theme. This was an interactive online session done with webinar participants.
- [Improved Software Development Processes with EventStorming](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Qgz19vaXYw) - This one gets into System Design and some recommendations for greenfield (brand new) versus brownfield (legacy) development.
